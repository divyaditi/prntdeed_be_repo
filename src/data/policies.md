# PrintFlow — Subscription Plans and Policies

## Overage Policy

If a Pro account exceeds 100 jobs in a billing month, each additional job is billed at $3.00. Starter accounts cannot exceed 10 jobs per month; a hard block is applied after the 10th job until the next billing cycle or until the account upgrades.

---

## Storage Policy

Files are retained in your account storage for 12 months after upload. After 12 months, files are automatically deleted. If you need longer retention, contact support. Enterprise accounts can negotiate a custom retention period.

When your storage quota is exceeded, new uploads are blocked until you delete existing files or upgrade your plan.

---

## Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your subscription at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you retain access to your plan features until then. There are no cancellation fees.

After cancellation, your account converts to a read-only state for 30 days (you can view job history and download invoices, but cannot place new jobs). After 30 days, the account and all stored files are permanently deleted.

---

## Uptime SLA

PrintFlow targets 99.9% monthly uptime for the portal and API. Planned maintenance windows are announced at least 48 hours in advance via status.printflow.io and email.

Enterprise customers may negotiate a custom uptime SLA with financial penalties for breach. The standard Starter and Pro plans do not include financial remedies for downtime.

---

## Data and Security

- All data is stored in the US-East-1 AWS region by default. Enterprise customers may request EU or APAC data residency.
- Files in transit are encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher.
- Files at rest are encrypted with AES-256.
- PrintFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified.
- GDPR data processing agreements (DPA) are available for all plans on request.
- HIPAA BAAs are available for Enterprise accounts only.

---

## Acceptable Use

PrintFlow may not be used to produce:
- Content that infringes third-party copyrights or trademarks
- Counterfeit currency, identification documents, or branded goods
- Obscene or illegal material
- Materials targeting minors in a harmful way

Violations result in immediate account suspension and forfeiture of any balance on file.

---

## Intellectual Property

You retain full ownership of all files you upload to PrintFlow. PrintFlow does not use your files for any purpose other than fulfilling your print orders. PrintFlow does not share your files with third parties except as required to complete production (e.g., sending to a contracted print facility).

---

## Privacy

PrintFlow collects account information, billing data, and usage logs. Usage data (job history, API call logs) is retained for 24 months. Full privacy policy: printflow.io/privacy.
